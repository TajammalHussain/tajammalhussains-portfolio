problem: >
  Supplier payments, subcontractor payment certificates and CIS payment
  sheets were produced through manual steps and manual approval chains. Each
  document had to be assembled by hand, passed between people for sign-off,
  and reconciled before payment, and the process depended on a dedicated
  administrative role to keep it moving.
constraints: >
  Subcontractor payments carry CIS, HMRC and BACS requirements, so the
  calculations and outputs had to be correct and traceable rather than
  approximately right. Approvals needed to support multiple authorisation
  levels and a signature that could be captured digitally instead of on
  paper.
approach: >
  I designed the suite as a set of structured digital workflows on Zoho
  Creator, scripted in Deluge. Each workflow replaces a manual approval chain
  with defined stages: submission, multi-level authorisation, e-signature and
  payment output. The CIS, HMRC and BACS logic is built into the workflow
  itself so the correct figures and file outputs are produced by the system
  rather than assembled by hand.
architecture: >
  Three linked workflow applications (supplier payments, subcontractor
  payment certificates, CIS payment sheets) share the same pattern:
  structured records, staged authorisation, captured e-signature, and generated
  output documents.
outcome: >
  The workflows eliminated a full administrative role and replaced manual
  approval chains with structured digital workflows including e-signature and
  multi-level authorisation.
